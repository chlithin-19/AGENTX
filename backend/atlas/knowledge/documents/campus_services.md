## 1. Information Technology & Wi-Fi Services
The University IT Helpdesk provides campus-wide high-speed Wi-Fi access (SSID: `CampusNet_Secure`), institutional email provisioning (`@campus.edu`), and student software licenses (MATLAB, Microsoft Office 365, GitHub Student Developer Pack).
- **Location:** Academic Block A, Room 102
- **Timings:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Support Contact:** `it-support@campus.edu` | Ext: 4102

## 2. Campus Transportation & Shuttle Schedule
Free battery-operated shuttle carts connect the Main Security Gate, Academic Block A & B, Student Activity Center, and Hostel Zones every 15 minutes.
- **Service Hours:** 6:30 AM to 9:30 PM daily
- **Route 1:** Gate 1 → Academic Block A → Central Library → SAC
- **Route 2:** SAC → Science Block → Hostels → Gate 2

## 3. Student Health Center & Emergency Care
The Campus Health Center offers 24/7 basic medical triage, emergency beds, resident medical staff, and free essential medicines.
- **Emergency Ambulance:** Extension 108 or Contact Gate 1 Security Desk
- **Regular Consultation Hours:** 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM
