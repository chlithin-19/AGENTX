## 1. Company Tiers & Eligibility Criteria
The Training and Placement Cell (T&P) categorizes campus recruitment drives into three tiers:
- **Super Dream Tier (> ₹12 LPA):** Requires CGPA ≥ 7.50, zero active backlogs, and verified proficiency scores in coding or system design.
- **Dream Tier (₹7 LPA to ₹12 LPA):** Requires CGPA ≥ 6.50, at most 1 historic backlog (cleared), and mandatory internship/project completion.
- **Core / Standard Tier (< ₹7 LPA):** Requires CGPA ≥ 6.00 with eligibility criteria specified by the participating corporate partner.

## 2. One Student One Job Policy
- Upon receiving a formal letter of intent or offer from a Tier 1 or Tier 2 corporate partner, a student is deemed placed.
- Students holding a Standard Tier offer remain eligible to participate exclusively in Dream and Super Dream campus drives.
- Acceptance of a Super Dream offer constitutes final placement, and the student's campus profile is sealed from subsequent drives.

## 3. Placement Ethics & Verification
- Fabricating internship duration, skills, mock interview scores, or project contributions results in immediate debarment from the placement season and disciplinary referral.
- Attendance at scheduled mock interviews, aptitude tests, and pre-placement talks (PPT) is mandatory for registered candidates.
