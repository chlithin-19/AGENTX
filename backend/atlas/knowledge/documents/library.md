## 1. Library Operating Hours & Schedule
The Central Library is located in the Central Library Building (CLB).
- **General Timings & Weekdays:** Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM (closes at 10:00 PM on weekdays).
- **Sunday Timings:** 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM (closes at 5:00 PM).
- **Examination Extended Hours:** During pre-exam and examination weeks, the Ground Floor Air-Conditioned Reading Hall remains open until 12:00 Midnight.

## 2. Circulation & Borrowing Rules
- **Undergraduate Students:** Entitled to borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for an initial duration of 14 days, renewable once online if no hold exists.
- **Overdue Charges:** ₹2 per day per volume after the grace period.
- **Digital Reference Access:** Students can access IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, and ScienceDirect using their `@campus.edu` institutional login credentials.

## 3. Library Facilities
- **Digital OPAC Kiosks:** Available on Floors 1 and 2 to search catalog items, call numbers, and shelf locations.
- **Discussion Rooms:** 6 sound-proof group study rooms available on Floor 2. Can be booked 24 hours in advance at the help counter.
