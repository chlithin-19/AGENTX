## 1. Attendance Requirements & Detention Rules
- **Standard Threshold:** A student is eligible to appear in the end-semester examinations only if they maintain a minimum aggregate attendance of **75%** across all registered courses.
- **Attendance Condonation (65% to 74.9%):** The Academic Dean may condone attendance shortages between 65% and 74.9% strictly on valid medical grounds, bereavement, or approved representation of the institution in state/national sporting or technical events. A formal condonation fee and supporting documentation must be submitted 14 days before exam commencement.
- **Detention (< 65%):** Any student with aggregate attendance below **65%** is summarily detained and cannot appear for end-semester examinations. The student must re-register for the respective semester courses when offered next.

## 2. Grading Scale & CGPA Evaluation
- **10-Point Scale:** The institution computes performance using the 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system:
  - `O` (Outstanding): 10 Grade Points (Marks ≥ 90%)
  - `A+` (Excellent): 9 Grade Points (Marks 80-89%)
  - `A` (Very Good): 8 Grade Points (Marks 70-79%)
  - `B+` (Good): 7 Grade Points (Marks 60-69%)
  - `B` (Above Average): 6 Grade Points (Marks 55-59%)
  - `C` (Pass): 5 Grade Points (Marks 50-54%)
  - `F` (Fail): 0 Grade Points (Marks < 50%)
- **Degree Eligibility:** Successful graduation requires an overall minimum CGPA of 5.0 with zero active backlogs at the time of final degree audit.

## 3. Backlog Clearance & Re-Evaluation
- **Supplementary Examinations:** Conducted within 45 days after end-semester result announcements for students with active backlogs (`F` grade).
- **Answer Script Verification:** Students may apply for answer script photocopy verification and formal re-evaluation within 10 days of grade publication via the Student Portal.
