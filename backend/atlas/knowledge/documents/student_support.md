# Student Support & Academic Advisory Guide (Demo Document)

> **Document Type:** Student Support Handbook (Demo)  
> **Effective Year:** 2026  
> **Source ID:** `doc_student_support_2026`  

## 1. Academic Mentorship Program
Every undergraduate student is assigned a Faculty Advisor within their home academic department (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil).
- Students may schedule bi-weekly advisory sessions to review course selection, attendance shortfalls, laboratory progression, and backlog clearance strategies.
- Office hours for advisors are posted outside departmental staff rooms and available on the campus portal.

## 2. Training and Placement Cell (T&P)
The Training & Placement Cell assists students with internship placements, campus recruitment preparation, and career counseling.
- **Location:** Student Activity Center (SAC), 2nd Floor
- **Services:** Mock interviews, resume screening, coding assessment bootcamps, and soft skill workshops.
- **Contact:** `placements@campus.edu`

## 3. Student Wellness & Counseling Services
A confidential psychological counseling and emotional wellness cell is available for all registered students.
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- **Location:** Health Center Annex, Room 104
