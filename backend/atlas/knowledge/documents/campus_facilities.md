## 1. Laboratory & Research Facilities
- **Computer Lab 204:** Academic Block A, 2nd Floor. Houses 60 high-performance workstations with Ubuntu Linux/Windows dual boot, high-lumen ceiling projector, and gigabit wired network. Used for CS/IT lab courses and hackathon sprints.
- **High-Performance Computing (HPC) Center:** Science Block, 3rd Floor. GPU clusters for deep learning, scientific simulation, and faculty research.
- **Advanced Electronics Lab:** Academic Block B, 1st Floor. Digital oscilloscopes, FPGA development boards, and embedded systems toolkits.

## 2. Sports & Recreational Facilities
- **Indoor Sports Hall:** Student Activity Center (SAC). Features 4 synthetic badminton courts, 6 table tennis tables, chess and carrom lounges.
- **Gymnasium & Fitness Center:** SAC Basement. Modern cardio and resistance training equipment, open 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM & 4:30 PM - 8:30 PM.
- **Outdoor Athletic Grounds:** Full-size cricket/football turf with floodlights for evening inter-departmental tournaments.

## 3. Maintenance Reporting & Facility Support
Any issue with classroom projectors, air conditioning, plumbing, or laboratory electrical fixtures should be reported via the Campus Agent or to the Facilities Maintenance Helpdesk at Ext: 3100.
