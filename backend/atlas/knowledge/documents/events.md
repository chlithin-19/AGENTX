## 2. Event Organization & Venue Booking Procedures
Student clubs and technical societies wishing to organize workshops, guest lectures, or hackathons must adhere to the following protocol:
1. Submit an event request proposal at least 7 working days prior to the proposed date via the Event Agent.
2. Ensure the venue requested has adequate capacity (e.g. Lab 204 max 60 persons, Seminar Hall 1 max 250 persons).
3. Secure faculty coordinator endorsement before financial reimbursement or external speaker entry passes are issued.
4. All campus events must conclude by 9:30 PM unless explicit overnight permission is granted by the Dean of Student Affairs.
